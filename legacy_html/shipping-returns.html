Shipping & Returns

Shipping Policy

We offer complimentary standard shipping on all orders over $100. For international shipping, rates are calculated at checkout.

All orders are processed within 2-3 business days. You will receive a tracking number once your order has been dispatched.

Returns & Exchanges

Due to the artisanal nature of our fragrances, we can only accept returns on unopened and unused products within 14 days of receipt.

To initiate a return, please contact our concierge at mlapandco@gmail.com.

Damaged Goods

If your order arrives damaged, please notify us immediately with photographs of the packaging and product for a replacement.
