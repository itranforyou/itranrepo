A Legacy of Silence

Born in the Heart of Kannauj

For nearly a century, Makhulal Ayodhya Prasad & Co has been the custodian of India's olfactory heritage. Nestled in the ancient city of Kannauj, often called the 'Grasse of the East', our family has perfected the art of traditional hydro-distillation.

Scented Silence is the contemporary evolution of this legacy—a bridge between ancient craftsmanship and the modern minimal silhouette. We believe that true luxury speaks in a whisper, not a shout.
