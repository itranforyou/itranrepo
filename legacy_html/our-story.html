Our Story

Five generations. One city. A thousand flowers distilled into silence.

The Beginning

Born in Kannauj,
the Perfume Capital

Nestled on the banks of the sacred Ganga, the city of Kannauj has produced attars for over a thousand years. It is here, in 1887, that our founder — Seth Makhulal Ayodhya Prasad — set up his first copper deg and began what would become a legacy spanning five generations.

His method was the ancient deg-bhapka process: flower petals loaded into a sealed copper pot, heated over a slow wood fire, steam carried through bamboo pipes into a receiving vessel submerged in cold water. The resulting oil — pure attar — would be collected drop by precious drop.

A single kilogram of Rose Attar requires over 300 kilograms of Damask rose petals, all harvested before sunrise. This is not manufacturing. This is devotion.

Through the Decades

A Legacy Told in Time

1887

The First Deg

Seth Makhulal Ayodhya Prasad lights the first fire under a copper deg in Kannauj. His first attar — a pure Rose of Taif — is gifted to the Nawab of Lucknow.

1920

The Sandalwood Era

The second generation introduces Mysore Sandalwood as a base for all their attars, creating a house signature of warmth and depth that endures to this day.

1962

The Oud Discovery

A trade journey to Assam introduces our family to Agarwood (Oud). The third generation begins distilling the first Indian-Assamese Oud attar, a rarity that becomes the house's most celebrated creation.

1995

Global Reach

The fourth generation begins exporting to connoisseurs in the Middle East and Europe, introducing the ancient wisdom of Indian attar to a new world audience.

2024

Scented Silence is Born

The fifth generation reimagines the legacy for the modern world — launching Scented Silence as a curated digital atelier, bringing 137 years of olfactory mastery to those who seek quiet luxury.

What We Stand For

Our Philosophy

eco

Ethically Sourced

Rose petals from Taif, vetiver roots from Haiti, jasmine from Grasse, sandalwood from Mysore. Every ingredient has a story and an address.

handyman

Handcrafted Always

Our copper degs have not been replaced since 1920. Every batch is stirred, tested, and approved by a family member before bottling begins.

do_not_disturb_alt

Zero Synthetics

We do not use synthetic aroma chemicals, stabilisers, or alcohol fillers. What you smell is what nature made — nothing more, nothing less.

schedule

Slow Production

We produce in small batches, never exceeding what our artisans can personally oversee. Some of our attars take 12 hours per batch. Patience is our ingredient.

A Note from the Family

"We distil not just flowers, but memory."

My great-great-grandfather used to say that a perfumer's greatest tool is not his nose, but his patience. The flowers decide when they are ready. The fire decides its own tempo. Our job is to simply be present and listen.

Each bottle of Scented Silence carries within it not just a fragrance, but the memory of a dawn harvest, the sound of copper vessels, and the quiet pride of five generations who believed that beauty does not need to announce itself.

— Aryan Prasad,
Fifth Generation, Scented Silence
